Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Greek Reader ("we", "us", "the app") is a reading assistant for Modern Greek. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's protected.

1. What We Collect

Email address

Used solely to authenticate you (sign-in) and to identify your account.

Learning progress data

Saved words, inflection practice history, and related study data tied to your account, so your progress is preserved between sessions and devices.

Uploaded text content

Documents or passages you upload for analysis, stored so you can revisit them.

IP address

We temporarily process your IP address in memory to enforce rate limits and prevent abuse. This is not persisted to disk or linked to your account, and is retained only as long as needed for this purpose.

We do not collect payment information, government ID, or any data beyond what's needed to run the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • ✓

    Your email is used only for authentication (e.g. login links or one-time codes).

  • ✓

    Your email is never used for marketing, newsletters, or any communication unrelated to authenticating your account.

  • ✓ Your progress and uploaded content are used only to provide the app's core functionality to you.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between your device and our servers.

Data is stored on servers located in Cape Town, South Africa.

A note for users in the European Union / EEA

Because our servers are located outside the EU/EEA, and South Africa does not currently hold a formal EU adequacy decision, transfers of your personal data to our servers are made on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as approved by the European Commission. If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app. Deletion is immediate, and your data is unrecoverable once deleted.

5. Security

Connections to Greek Reader are encrypted via TLS. Sensitive tokens are stored with industry-standard hashing. While no system is completely immune to breaches, we take reasonable technical measures to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions about this policy, please contact us at the email address in the footer below.

7. Children's Privacy

Greek Reader is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
